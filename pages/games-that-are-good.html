Games that are good

  • Deltarune: Funny indie RPG where nothing bad ever happens.

  • Undertale: Funny indie RPG where you choose whether bad happens.

  • Stardew Valley: Turns from a relaxing farming game into a capitalism simulator.

  • Portal 2: Puzzles and funny moments.
